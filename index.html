The studio

Small team, wide range.

Red Spider Studios is Brian Rossney — a VFX supervisor and 3D/design generalist with nearly 30 years' experience across TV/film, architecture, automotive, medical and science — working with a trusted bench of regular VFX collaborators for feature film work. The studio is built around previsualization, with recent previz work including 'Wednesday' Season 3 and 'Hivemind'. Alongside that, we run bespoke virtual-set builds in Unreal Engine and train the next wave of Unreal artists through our own courses.
