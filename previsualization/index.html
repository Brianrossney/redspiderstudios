Previsualization for film and episodic television.

Red Spider Studios is an Unreal Engine previz studio. We block sequences, solve camera moves and hand production a shot-by-shot plan before anyone books a crew. Recent previz work includes 'Wednesday' Season 3 and 'Hivemind'.

Previz for 'Andor' (Disney) by The 3rd Floor — shown as an example of how a sequence is blocked, shot and refined before a camera rolls.

What previz actually buys a production.

Previsualization is the stage where a sequence stops being a description and becomes something you can watch. Every department gets to plan against the same thing, and the expensive decisions get made before the expensive days start.
